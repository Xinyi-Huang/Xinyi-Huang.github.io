I am currently a postdoctoral fellow in Department of Earth, Environmental & Planetary Sciences ([<u>DEEPS</u>](https://deeps.brown.edu/)) at Brown University. 
My current advisors are [<u>Dr. Baylor Fox-Kemper</u>](https://fox-kemper.com/) and [<u>Dr. Emanuele Di Lorenzo</u>](https://www.ocean.brown.edu/) from DEEPS. 
Before coming to Brown, I was a postdoctoral fellow in Graduate Aerospace Laboratories ([<u>GALCIT</u>](https://www.galcit.caltech.edu/)) at Caltech. 
I earned my Ph.D. degree in Department of Mechanical Engineering at Pennsylvania State University. 
My research interest is to combine data-driven techniques and turbulence research with the assistance of physical insights. 

Rapidly growing AI for science community provides more than new tools for existing turbulence problems, but also new perspectives for understanding turbulent systems and the framework of turbulence research. 
I worked on the turbulence model development, tools for easy use of turbulence models, and measuring the capability of a turbulence model, which benefited our understanding of the canonical flows. 
My recent work at Brown University is futher targeting the real-world applications, which will directly benefit local communicty with a more accessible climate model. 
I welcome discussions on any topics on turbulence, climate modeling, and a broad range of fluid mechanics!
