Some of the key concepts in geophysical fluid dynamics, the details of which could be found in Baylor Fox-Kemper's [<u>EEPS 1820</u>](https://fox-kemper.com/classes/), Joern Callies's course notes for [<u>ESE130, ESE131</u>](https://jcallies.com/teaching/), Geoffrey K. Vallis's textbook *Atmospheric and Oceanic Fluid Dynamics*. 
I personally also found Baylor's Youtube channel videos on [<u>GEOL1820</u>](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL-c4w21QKNGiln8591rGCzT6iynxFmYkd) and Nick Hall's [<u>GFD course videos</u>](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLDrzzwhdpdSBiMLT5TZkySriC4QUhG4Wo) very helpful when I am learning GFD. 
A lot of other materials/textbook are very useful, but I am not listing as I did not read.
